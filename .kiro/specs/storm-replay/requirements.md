# Spec: Storm Replay (Graveyard of Storms)

## Requirements (EARS)
- **FR-SR-1:** WHEN a user selects a historical tombstone (Halloween 2003, Gannon May 2024, Nov 2025, Jan 2026), THE System SHALL run the live risk engine over archived spacecraft numbers and clearly display a `REPLAY` badge.
- **FR-SR-2:** WHEN replaying the May 10, 2024 Gannon storm (shock at 17:05 UTC, 714 km/s, Bz -47 nT, Kp 9.0), THE System SHALL output `Haunted` with an upstream L1 lead time of ~35 minutes.
